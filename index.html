⭕ Circles: Center, Radius, Diameter & Chord

d = 2 × r

A circle is a closed curved shape with 0 straight sides and 0 corners. Every point on the edge is the exact same distance from the Center!

Radius (r): Center to the edge (half of diameter).
Diameter (d): Straight across through Center (2 × r).
Chord: Connects 2 edge points (Diameter is the longest chord!).
Circumference: Total distance around the outside edge.
Radius (r) = 4 in | Diameter (d) = 8 in

📘 Beast Academy 1B (Current) Ch. 4–6

  • Ch. 8 — Algorithms: Stacking multi-digit numbers vertically, carrying 10 ones into 1 ten, and borrowing across tens and zeros (300 − 146).
  • Ch. 9 — Measurement & Perimeter: Indirect comparisons (if A > B and B > C, then A > C), balance scales, rulers, and polygon perimeter.
📕 Beast Academy 1D (Next Level) Ch. 10–12

🔢 MAP Mathematics Syllabus & Beast Academy 1B–1D Alignment
1. Operations & Algebraic Thinking

Two-step word problems; missing addends; BA 1B Making-10 & Compensation strategies; BA 1B Odds & Evens parity; number patterns & skip-counting series; arrays & BA 1D Working Backwards / Magic Squares.

2. Number & Operations

Place value up to 1,000; BA 1C flexible regrouping & vertical addition/subtraction algorithms with carrying/borrowing across zeros; BA 1B inequalities (<, >, =); rounding to nearest 10 & 100.

3. Measurement & Data

BA 1D Money & Coins (making change over $1.00); analog clocks to 5-minute intervals & elapsed time; BA 1C Measurement & Perimeter; inches, feet, yards, miles, cm, meters; bar/picture graphs & BA 1D Venn Diagrams.

4. Geometry & Circles

Circles & Curves (center, radius, diameter, chord, circumference, semicircle); BA 1D Fractions (halves, thirds, fourths); polygons (pentagon, hexagon, octagon); 3D faces/edges/vertices; symmetry & area tiling.

📚 MAP English Reading & Language Arts Syllabus (RIT 177 → 189+)
1. Literature & Informational Text

Inferencing character feelings & motives from text clues; cause & effect; chronological story sequence; main idea vs. supporting details; nonfiction text features (glossary, index, headings, captions); comparing two texts.

2. Vocabulary Use & Functions

Context-dependent multiple-meaning words (bark, bat, wave, light, watch); prefixes (un-, re-, pre-, dis-, mis-) & suffixes (-ful, -less, -er, -ly); context clues; word intensity series & analogies.

3. Foundational Skills & Phonics

Multisyllabic decoding & syllable counting; compound words; long vowel teams; diphthongs (ou/ow, oi/oy); r-controlled vowels; silent letters (kn, wr, mb); homophones (to/two/too, there/their/they're); inflectional spelling rules.

4. Language & Writing Mechanics

Commas in a series, dates, cities/states, and letters; apostrophes in contractions & singular/plural possessives; parts of speech (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns); irregular past-tense verbs & irregular plurals.
