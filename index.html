S3 • Life Science

Animal & Plant Habitat Adaptations

🦦 Ocean Kelp Forest

Sea Otters have super-thick fur (1 million hairs/sq in) to stay warm and wrap in kelp like an anchor while sleeping.

🌵 Arid Desert

Cactus Plants have thick waxy stems to store water and sharp spines to protect against thirsty animals.

🐻‍❄️ Arctic Tundra

Polar Bears & Seals have thick fat layers (blubber) and white fur for warmth and snow camouflage.
